WTA WTA02/attempt_002: identical 273/576 — the same classes (100 raw, 16 selected, 16 shared_base, 6 market CSVs; 38 JSON records; 100 training_keys.csv; progress files; stderr). Differing 303: the same leaves, plus `fit_wall_clock_seconds` *added* on every attempt record (the WTA02 revision did not measure it; the union always does — it enters no hash a forecast depends on).
Label reads: `labels.csv` read as history at `pipeline.label_subset` -> `chain.labels.LabelHistory.selected`, three call sites (table below).
Learned constants: the per-year calibration slopes and selected candidates (`selection/<year>/<learner>/<block>.json`), the shared-complexity slopes (`shared_base/...json`) and the market slopes (`market/<year>/calibration.json`), each recorded with its selection years, inclusive cutoff (30 December of `outer_year - 1`), row count and membership hash — all identical to the archive.
Fit cache: `<output>/fit_cache/<identity sha256>/` inside the stage's own output, created by the run; there is no external cache and the archive run tree holds none. A fresh run refits every attempt (ATP 110 fits in about four minutes; WTA 100). `report` and `reporting_config` do not touch it; they are fast because they only read hashed forecasts.
Open: nothing.

## Outcome reads

Every read goes through `LabelHistory` with a declared purpose and a year ceiling that refuses any requested key from a later season; the pipeline never opens the label file otherwise.

| Call site | `purpose` | Keys requested | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `run_raw_stage`, once per raw year | `training_fit` | `training_keys(year)`: aligned-primary rows with `match_date` in `[max(history_floor, year - training_window_years)-01-01, (year-1)-12-30]` | `year - 1` |
| `run_selection_stage`, once per target year | `past_selection_calibration` | `selection_keys(outer_year)`: aligned-primary rows of the `calibration_years_back` past years dated on or before `(outer_year-1)-12-30` | `outer_year - 1` |
| `run_selection_stage`, once per target year | `past_market_calibration` | the market-valid subset of the same past-year keys | `outer_year - 1` |

No read touches a target year's outcomes: every attempt record carries `outcome_labels_used_for_prediction: false` and every selection, shared-base and market record carries `outer_labels_used: false`, and the reporter re-checks both flags before scoring.
